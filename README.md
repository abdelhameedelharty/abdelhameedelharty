### Now

- **PhD research:** seamless indoor–outdoor positioning. I work on multi-sensor fusion (GNSS, UWB, IMU) and error modelling in the transition zone, where satellite signals fade at doors and passages and indoor systems take over.
- **Currently building:** a pilot GNSS + UWB + IMU dataset with smartphone raw GNSS logging, plus tools for single point positioning, signal quality and pedestrian dead reckoning (see the projects below).

### Research interests

- **Now:** seamless indoor–outdoor positioning; GNSS in degraded environments (smartphone raw GNSS, C/N0, multipath and NLOS); GNSS / UWB / IMU fusion; quality control and reliability (data snooping, outlier detection).
- **Earlier work:**
  - Gravity field and geoid determination for Egypt.
  - GRACE / GRACE-FO satellite gravimetry for terrestrial water storage.
  - Geo.AI: deep learning and CNNs in geoscience.
  - Geospatial data fusion and big-data analysis for climate studies.
  - GNSS data processing for geodetic and environmental applications.
