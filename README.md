## Hi, I'm Abdelhameed Elharty

PhD student at **Wuhan University**, working on **seamless indoor–outdoor positioning**. I study multi-sensor fusion (GNSS, UWB and IMU) and error modelling in the transition zone, the doors and passages where satellite signals fade and indoor systems take over.

My background is in geodesy and surveying: GNSS/RTK, total stations, coordinate transformations and least-squares adjustment.

### Research interests

- **GNSS in degraded environments:** single point positioning, signal quality (C/N0), multipath and NLOS, smartphone raw GNSS measurements.
- **Multi-sensor fusion:** GNSS / UWB / IMU, pedestrian dead reckoning, Kalman filtering.
- **Quality control:** Baarda data snooping, outlier detection, reliability of positioning.
- **Geodesy:** datum transformations, geoid models, map projections.
